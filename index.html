About

Background

I am a Research Associate in the Global Research Initiative for Sustainable Low-Carbon Unconventional Resources at the University of Calgary, and an Adjunct Research Fellow at Curtin Institute for Energy Transition in Australia. My work connects environmental policy, energy access, and sustainable development.

I study plastic waste and household energy, clean-energy transitions, and how place and socioeconomic conditions shape policy outcomes. I led a multi-country study of plastic burning as household fuel and have worked with more than 40 research partners.

My research has appeared in Nature Communications, Nature Cities, Energy Research & Social Science, and Cell Reports Sustainability. It has also been covered by outlets including The Guardian, The Times of India, and Mongabay.

I hold a PhD in Human Geography from the University of Queensland; an MSc in Climate Change from University College London; an MSc in Natural Resource Management and a BSc in Environmental Science from Tribhuvan University, Nepal.
